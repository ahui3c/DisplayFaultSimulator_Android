# Google Play Store listing (English, United States)

## App name

Display Fault Simulator

## Short description

Simulate screen lines, cracks, liquid damage, pixels, and animated faults

## Full description

Display Fault Simulator recreates common OLED and LCD failure visuals in a transparent overlay. It is designed for harmless pranks, video production, interface demonstrations, and display testing.

Highlights:
• Classic OLED green line, multiple pink lines, and custom line colors
• Spiderweb, radial-impact, corner-shatter, and multi-impact cracks
• OLED black spots, edge bleed, dead pixels, ghosting, scanlines, PWM bands, and tearing
• Built-in light, severe, and animated fault presets
• Adjustable position, width, opacity, glow, flicker, and gentle movement
• Direct dragging for lines and crack impact centers
• Countdown start, automatic stop, Quick Settings tile, and notification Stop action
• Optional shake, flip, charging, and unlock triggers

Every effect is visual only. The touch-through overlay does not capture the screen, read other apps, or intercept taps, swipes, and system gestures. Stop the effect at any time from the app, persistent notification, or Quick Settings tile.

The Display over other apps permission is required to show effects above the home screen and other apps. A visible foreground-service notification keeps the effect user-controlled and provides an immediate Stop action.

The app has no network access, ads, analytics, or tracking and does not collect or share personal data.

Important: This app only simulates visual faults. It cannot detect, diagnose, damage, or repair a physical display.
